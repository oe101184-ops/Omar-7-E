The person that created the sport is unknown but what we do know is is that England was the founder of the sport Modern football originated in 19th-century England, where a need for standardized rules arose from public school games with differing rules. This led to the formation of the Football Association in 1863, which established the rules that form the basis of association football today. However, ball-kicking games have existed across cultures for centuries, including ancient Greek and Roman games, and medieval "folk football" in Europe.
